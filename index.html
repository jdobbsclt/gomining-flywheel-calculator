Formula reference

Daily maintenance per TH (at 0% discount)

elec_0 = (kWh_rate × 24 × W/TH) / GMT_price / 1000 service_0 = service_constant / GMT_price

Actual daily maintenance (with your discount stack applied)

daily_GMT = TH × (elec_0 + service_0) × (1 − total_discount) weekly_OPEX_GMT = daily_GMT × 7

Locked / liquid GMT from displayed "days" (and back)

locked_GMT ≈ locked_days × TH × (elec_0 + service_0) liquid_GMT ≈ liquid_days × TH × (elec_0 + service_0) locked_days = locked_GMT ÷ (TH × (elec_0 + service_0)) (when you type GMT instead)

GoMining's "days" figure is always computed at the 0% discount rate — that's why this conversion doesn't use your discount stack, even though your actual weekly bill does. You can enter either days or GMT; the one you edited last is kept, and the other is derived from it.

Coverage & breakeven

weekly_dividend_GMT = locked_GMT × APR ÷ 52 coverage % = weekly_dividend_GMT ÷ weekly_OPEX_GMT × 100 required_locked_GMT = (target% × weekly_OPEX_GMT × 52) ÷ APR breakeven_APR = (target% × weekly_OPEX_GMT × 52) ÷ current_locked_GMT

Dividends and OPEX are compared in GMT terms directly, so GMT price only matters for converting to USD for display — not for the coverage math itself.
